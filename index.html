A Little Reminder for You 💜

Hey, birthday girl! Before you leave, here's a little reminder na I hope you'll carry with you, not just today, but even on the days when life feels a little heavier than usual.

Hindi laging madaling malaman kung saan ka ba talaga papunta sa buhay. Sometimes, things don't turn out the way we imagined them to be, and there are days when we start questioning if we're even on the right path. And that's okay. You don't have to figure everything out all at once.

Hindi mo kailangang pilitin ang sarili mo sa isang bagay na unti-unting umuubos sa'yo just to prove that you're strong. Your health matters. Your peace matters. Your well-being matters, too. It's okay to take a step back, to rest, to change direction, or to start again. Hindi ibig sabihin no'n na sumuko ka. Sometimes, choosing what's healthier for you takes more courage than staying where you are.

And through it all, don't forget to bring your worries to God. You may not always understand where He's leading you, but you can trust Him one step at a time. You don't need to have your whole life figured out today.

“Be still, and know that I am God.”
Psalm 46:10

As you enter another year of your life, I pray na bigyan ka ni Lord ng wisdom sa mga decisions mo, courage to make the changes you need, strength for the difficult days, and peace habang hinahanap mo kung saan ka talaga magg-grow.

May you find a path that doesn't just help you get through each day, but allows you to live with peace, purpose, and joy.

You are allowed to begin again. You are allowed to choose peace. And you are allowed to trust God's timing, even when you don't have all the answers yet.

Praying for a year filled with God's grace, new beginnings, and better days ahead. Always remember to take care of yourself, too. God bless you! 💜
